# Client agreements — which one to use

Understory's contract set, and the rule for picking the right one. These are
**templates**, not legal advice. Have a lawyer review them once before the first
signature, then reuse them. Fill in every `{{PLACEHOLDER}}` and resolve every item in
the **Open questions** block at the top of each file before you send it to a client.

## The set

| Document | File | Use it when |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery Agreement | `discovery-agreement.md` | A small, time-boxed first engagement: a paid discovery or pilot, roughly the shape of our 10-hour pro bono run. One short document that carries both the terms and the scope. |
| Master Services Agreement (MSA) | `master-services-agreement.md` | Work is larger, or a client is coming back for a second thing. Sign it once. It sets the ground rules and nothing about a specific project. |
| Statement of Work (SOW) | `statement-of-work.md` | Every project run under an MSA. It names the deliverables, price, dates, and acceptance for that one engagement. Sign one per project. |

## How to choose

```
First engagement, small and time-boxed?  -> Discovery Agreement
Larger project, or a returning client?    -> MSA + SOW
Another project with a client who already signed an MSA? -> just a new SOW
```

- The MSA and SOW are a pair. The MSA is the framework; the SOW is the work. An MSA
  with no SOW commits no one to anything.
- The Discovery Agreement is a complete, standalone contract. It does not require an MSA.
- When a Discovery Agreement client grows into ongoing work, move them to an MSA plus SOW
  for the next engagement.

## Why we lead with the Discovery Agreement, not the MSA

Presenting a full MSA for a three-week discovery reads as heavier than the work. The
Discovery Agreement is proportional: fast to sign, warm, and complete on its own. The MSA
earns its place the moment work scales or repeats.

## Confidentiality

We already have a mutual NDA (the MNDA in Drive; see the "When to Pull the NDA" cheat
sheet for when to send it). To avoid two documents saying different things about the same
subject:

- If an MNDA is already signed with the client, the MSA and Discovery Agreement **point to
  it** rather than restating confidentiality terms.
- If no MNDA is signed, use the short confidentiality section included in each template.
