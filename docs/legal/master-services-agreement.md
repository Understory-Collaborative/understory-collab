# Master Services Agreement (MSA)

The framework agreement, signed once per client. It sets the ground rules and says nothing
about a specific project. Each project runs under a Statement of Work (see
`statement-of-work.md`). An MSA with no SOW commits no one to any work.

## Master Services Agreement

**Effective Date:** {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}

This Master Services Agreement (the "Agreement") is between webs on the webs, a Nevada
{{ENTITY_TYPE — e.g., LLC}} doing business as Understory Collaborative ("Understory," "we,"
or "us") and {{CLIENT_LEGAL_NAME}} (the "Client," "you"). Understory and the Client are each
a "party" and together the "parties."

### 1. How this Agreement works

This Agreement sets the terms that govern all services we provide to you. It does not by
itself commit either party to any particular work. Each engagement is described in a
separate Statement of Work ("SOW") that both parties sign. Each SOW is governed by this
Agreement. If a SOW conflicts with this Agreement, this Agreement controls, except where the
SOW expressly says it is changing a specific term of this Agreement for that engagement.

### 2. Services

We will perform the services described in each SOW (the "Services") and deliver the items it
lists (the "Deliverables"). We will perform the Services in a professional and workmanlike
manner and with reasonable skill and care.

### 3. Client responsibilities

You will give us timely access to the people, systems, materials, and decisions the
Services depend on, and a reasonably responsive point of contact. Our timelines and
estimates assume this. If your delays hold up the work, related dates and estimates shift
accordingly, and we are not responsible for missing a date for that reason.

### 4. Change control

Either party may propose a change to a SOW. A change takes effect only when both parties
agree to it in writing (email is fine), including any effect on scope, price, or timeline.

### 5. Fees and payment

- Fees, rates, and the payment schedule are set in each SOW. A SOW may be fixed price, priced
  per iteration, or a monthly retainer.
- We invoice as the SOW states. Invoices are due within {{NET_DAYS — default: 15}} days of
  the invoice date.
- Late amounts may accrue interest at {{LATE_INTEREST — default: 1.5%}} per month or the
  maximum allowed by law, whichever is less. If an invoice is more than {{GRACE — default:
  30}} days overdue, we may suspend the Services after giving you written notice and a chance
  to cure.
- Fees do not include taxes. You are responsible for any sales, use, or similar taxes, other
  than taxes on our income.
- {{Optional: Reasonable, pre-approved out-of-pocket expenses are billed at cost.}}

### 6. Term

This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues until either party ends it under
section 7. It stays in effect for as long as any SOW is active, even past a termination
notice, until that SOW is finished or separately ended.

### 7. Termination

- Either party may end this Agreement for convenience with {{NOTICE — default: thirty (30)
  days'}} written notice, provided no SOW is active; if a SOW is active, the notice takes
  effect when that SOW ends unless the parties agree otherwise.
- Either party may end this Agreement or any SOW if the other materially breaches and does
  not cure the breach within {{CURE — default: fifteen (15)}} days of written notice.
- Either party may end a single SOW under its own termination terms without ending this
  Agreement.
- On termination, you will pay us for all Services performed and non-cancelable costs
  committed through the effective date of termination.

### 9. Intellectual property

- **Deliverables.** On our receipt of full payment for the SOW under which a Deliverable is
  created, we assign to you our rights in that Deliverable as delivered, for your use.
- **Background IP.** We keep all rights in everything we bring to the work that predates or
  sits outside a SOW: our methods, frameworks, tools, templates, and know-how ("Background
  IP"), including any improvements to it. To the extent our Background IP is embedded in a
  Deliverable, we grant you a perpetual, non-exclusive, worldwide, paid-up license to use it
  as part of that Deliverable for your internal business purposes.
- **Our general knowledge.** Nothing in this Agreement stops us from using the general skills,
  knowledge, and experience we develop in the course of the work.
- **Marketing.** We may describe our work for you in general terms in our portfolio and
  marketing, without disclosing your confidential information or naming you, unless the SOW
  says otherwise or you ask us in writing not to.

- **Amendments.** Any change to this Agreement must be in writing and signed by both parties.
- **Severability.** If any part is unenforceable, the rest stays in effect.
