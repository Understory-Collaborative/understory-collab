# Voice gate — Understory Collaborative

The standard every line of website copy passes through before anyone sees it. It exists so we stop rewriting copy that drifted into slop. Run each line against it as you write, not after.

**Rules are defaults, not a cage. webs approves exceptions**, and once approved they go in the log at the bottom and stay. Do not "fix" an approved exception later.

This gate governs the **website**. Chat and non-website artifacts follow `CLAUDE.md` instead.

---

## Register — the feel

- A seasoned CTO telling you the truth over coffee. Plain, unhurried, never selling.
- Educate, do not pitch. Lead with the true observation, then the point.
- Complete sentences. Calm tone. No ad-fragment punch.
- Use contractions. "you're," "what's," "we'll." The stilted separate-word version reads cold. The voice is a person talking, not a policy.

---

## Blocklist — an automatic fail

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Em dashes | (use periods, commas, colons, or parentheses) |
| Self-certifying virtue words | honest, honestly, the truth is, straight, frankly, real talk |
| AI-slop vocabulary | seamless, cutting-edge, game-changer, best-in-class, unlock, elevate, empower, leverage (as a verb), dive in, revolutionary, synergy, end-to-end, a "read" on the situation, "it lands on you" / "it lands" |
| False contrast | no trailing ", not X" and no leading "not X, but Y." State the positive directly. |
| Cleverness that performs | wordplay or punch that draws attention to itself |
| Stock names & ability metaphors | "Sarah Chen"; sensory or ability metaphors used loosely |

---

## Form

- Sentence case headings ("What we do"). Title case is a flagged exception.
- Oxford comma. Lowercase "webs" and lowercase UI labels.
- The forest/fire metaphor lives in words, kept subtle. Never go literal (no flames, no parachutes).

---

## Selling rules

- **Don't put your own stuff down to sell the next thing.** "No quiz required" negs the quiz. Sell each rung on what it is.
- **A form starts a conversation. It doesn't scope a job.** Never promise an outcome, a price, or a scope from a form submission.
- **Don't name things that don't exist yet.** No podcast until there is a podcast.

---

## Sounds like / doesn't

| Slop | Fails on | Gated |
|---|---|---|
| "Something's on fire." | ad punch, performs | "Most engineering orgs have a fire they've stopped noticing." |
| "Get a straight read on how bad it is." | self-certifying ("straight") | "The quiz tells you how serious it is in about two minutes." |
| "We deliver end-to-end solutions that empower your team to unlock its full potential." | slop stack, no content | "We take the work in whatever shape it arrives: a project that's slipped, a team that's struggling, or an AI rollout that's gotten away from you." |
| "Ready to grow together? Let's transform your ideas into reality." | empty pitch | "You have the report. Office hours is where you decide what to do about the most urgent part of it." |
| "Fill out the form and we'll get back to you ASAP!" | lead-form filler, reads cheap | "Tell us what's going on in your own words, and we'll set up a conversation to work out the scope together." |

## The website's three registers

From `design-system/README.md`. Same voice, tuned to the surface:

- **Peer diagnosis** (home, quiz, CTAs): direct second person, real questions, no sell.
- **Field guide** (white papers): patient and educational. A guide that says "that's where we come in" has broken character.
- **Quiet brand** (taglines, hero, metadata): understated, soft-spoken.
